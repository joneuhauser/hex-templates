# Template data provenance

The repository MIT license applies to the new solver, tools, and site code. It does not replace the provenance or license of third-party template data.

- **H36-class0:** optimized geometry of the Xiang–Liu (2018) connectivity, https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.09415 . The public mesh used for comparison is https://kilian.ac/posts/meshing-game/meshes/pyramid.mesh . Vertex labels and interior geometry differ from that source; connectivity is isomorphic.
- **H44-class16:** original coordinates and connectivity of Verhetsel–Pellerin–Remacle, https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01022 . Download linked directly from https://www.hextreme.eu/paper-schneiders-44/ : https://www.hextreme.eu/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/verhetsel-imr2018-slides/meshes/schneiders-44.mesh . The asset is retained byte-for-byte (SHA-256 `8c61a75d882c0935e1e1412cb7deb582cf7ed3e06d774c3e02a7baf0240eccb1`). No snapping, normalization or optimization is applied. The viewer alone uses the rigid rotation `(x,y,z) -> (x,-z,y)`. The original file omits boundary quads, so these are inferred from connectivity. Its rounded boundary is audited with a separate 5e-6 coordinate / 1e-5 absolute volume tolerance; determinant positivity is certified exactly on the original coordinates. Its measured geometric symmetry is C1 despite its two-mirror connectivity. The upstream GPL notice is retained in COPYING-HEXTREME.txt and with the solver’s published replay fixtures.
- **H88-yamakawa:** Yamakawa and Shimada, *88-Element solution to Schneiders’ pyramid hex-meshing problem*, IJNMBE 26 (2010), 1700–1712, https://doi.org/10.1002/cnm.1256 . Source member `results/schneiders_88.mesh` of the same Hextreme archive. Its upstream distribution license is retained in COPYING-HEXTREME.txt. Connectivity is unchanged except for vertex relabeling. Coordinates are transformed by `(x,y,z) -> (x,-z,y*sqrt(2)/1.41421)`, and 18 rounded boundary vertices are restored to the prescribed pyramid (maximum correction 3.000008e-6). Interior coordinates are otherwise unchanged after the affine transformation. The catalog records the original file hash and exported file hash. The resulting asset passes the independent whole-cell positivity and topology audit.
- **Other H36–H48 entries:** distinct connectivities generated by the solver and coordinate optimizer. The catalog records the selected embedding and its SHA-256. These generated assets and their measurements are distributed under the repository MIT license.

All downloaded `.mesh` assets in the archive have been revalidated locally. Sampled quality measurements are not global-extremum certificates. Published-paper attribution is independent of geometric optimization applied to the displayed meshes.
